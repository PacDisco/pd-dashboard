Sources. Funnel, sessions and attribution are read live from HubSpot and Jotform on every load via /api/sales-funnel-data. Ad spend comes from /api/marketing-spend and is entered by hand, because neither Google Ads nor Meta is connected to this stack. Definitions. Lead = contact created with Company Tag “Pacific Discovery”. Opportunity = deal entering an Application Fee Received stage. Sale = deal entering a Deposit Paid stage, counted once in the month the deposit landed. Excluded. College Credit deals, test records, and the Unearthed Education pipeline. Health warning. Original Source is unreliable while records arriving through Zapier land as “Offline Sources” with their real channel stripped. Inside Offline Sources reads the real channel back out of original_source_drill_down_3/4/5, where the form's utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign survive — but only for records that carried a UTM in the first place, which is why a large remainder stays unrecovered. Fixing the source at the point of capture is still the real repair; this panel is a read of the damage, not a substitute. Leads and sales in these tables are not a cohort. A deposit received in July usually belongs to a lead created months earlier — the gap-year cycle runs long. So read the Leads, Opps and Sales columns as three counts that happened in the window, and compare the rankings between them. Dividing one by the other looks like a conversion rate and isn't one, which is why the page doesn't offer that number. On the two attribution panels. They cover different populations. The cookie panel covers every lead. The self-reported panel covers only leads who submitted an application, because that is the only place the question is asked — the count underneath it says how many that is. Comparing their totals is a category error; comparing their rankings is the point.
