Sources. Funnel, sessions and attribution are read live from HubSpot and Jotform on every load via /api/sales-funnel-data. Ad spend comes from /api/marketing-spend and is entered by hand, because neither Google Ads nor Meta is connected to this stack. Definitions. Lead = contact created with Company Tag “Pacific Discovery”. Opportunity = deal entering an Application Fee Received stage. Sale = deal entering a Deposit Paid stage, counted once in the month the deposit landed. Excluded. College Credit deals, test records, and the Unearthed Education pipeline. Health warning. Original Source is unreliable while records arriving through Zapier land as “Offline Sources” with their real channel stripped. Where the two attribution panels disagree, the application-form answer is the more credible of the two.
