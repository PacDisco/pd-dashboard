Get started

Add yourself below and click Connect calendar. The first calendar connected becomes the host: invites come from it and check-ins are saved to it. Until then, the portal form shows "not set up yet".

To change who's on a booked check-in, open it in Google Calendar → Edit → Guests. Anyone on the invite can do this, on any domain. For a weekly series, choose "All events".
